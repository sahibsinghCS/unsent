Label cheat sheet
asked_directly must NAME suicide / killing yourself / ending your life / not wanting to be alive / self-harm in a question. "are you ok?" = checked_in only; "you're not gonna do something dumb, right?" = NOT asked_directly.
referred_adult names a trusted adult (counselor, parent, teacher...). referred_988 mentions 988 / a crisis line. "I won't tell... except the counselor" = referred_adult, not promised_secrecy.
dismissed minimizes, jokes, or changes the subject. advice_dump = "just / you should" fixing before listening (not "get help"). promised_secrecy = agrees to keep it secret.
Spanish / typos / slang: label the same way. "huh?" or "ok" = no labels.
